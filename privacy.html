Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

1. Overview

This website (asvinang.github.io) is a static, personal portfolio showcasing my engineering projects, professional experience, and automation stack. Because this site is designed with transparency and simplicity in mind, it is committed to respecting your privacy.

2. Data Collection & Cookies

This site does not use cookies, local storage tracking identifiers, or any backend systems to collect, track, or sell your Personal Identifiable Information (PII). Any interactions on this site, such as viewing projects or revealing contact info, occur purely inside your local browser.

3. Third-Party Services

To ensure a premium and reliable visual experience, this site loads specific external assets. When you load these resources, your IP address and user-agent string are visible to these providers:

  • Google Fonts: Used for typography layouts, hosted by Google.
  • Lucide Icons: Rendered dynamically via unpkg.com (a public CDN).
  • GitHub Pages: This site is hosted by GitHub Inc., which may record web request log files for security and platform operation purposes.

4. Visitor Counter

We use Abacus (https://abacus.jasoncameron.dev), a privacy-first, zero-tracking hit counter service.

  • It increments a simple numeric counter whenever the page is loaded.
  • No cookies, IP logs, geo-location statistics, or visitor profile databases are created or stored by this service.
  • It serves only to provide a simple visual log of page views.

5. Contact & Anti-Spam Reveal

The email address and phone number displayed in the contact section are obfuscated by default to prevent web scraping and automated spam. When you click to reveal them, the decryption and layout adjustment are done entirely in your browser using local client-side JavaScript. No contact information or click history is transmitted to any server.

6. Contact Details

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via the contact options on the homepage.
